About the room

Kelsi's Beauty Room is a nail and beauty studio specialising in nails, brow treatments, lash lifts and skincare. Every appointment is unhurried and detail-focused, whether you're after a subtle brow lamination or a full set of hand-painted nail art.

Booking is simple — pick your treatment, choose a time that suits you, and Kelsi will take care of the rest.

Please note: a patch test is required 24–48 hours before any new lash or brow tint treatment.
